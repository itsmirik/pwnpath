# RSA: Same Modulus

The same plaintext message was encrypted twice under the **same modulus** `n`,
but with two different public exponents `e1` and `e2`. You are given `n`, both
exponents, and both ciphertexts `c1`, `c2` in `rsa.txt`.

You do not need to factor `n`. Recover the message.

Flag format: `byuctf{...}`

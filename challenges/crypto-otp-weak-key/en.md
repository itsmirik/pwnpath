# One Time Pad

> I just learned about an encryption algorithm that is impossible to break, so
> I'll give you the ciphertext because I know you can't break it. In fact, I'm
> so confident you can't crack it that I'll even tell you I used my password as
> the secret key!

Ciphertext:

```
SKWDHAICUG
```

The cipher combines each plaintext letter with a letter of the key (a classic
one-time-pad style shift over the alphabet A-Z). Recover the plaintext.

Flag format: `byuctf{ANENGLSHPHRSE}`

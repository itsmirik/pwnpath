# Origins of Legoclones

For reasons completely incomprehensible to mankind, you've become the sworn enemy of a CTF organizer who goes by "Legoclones". To defeat him, you decide to dig into his origins. Here's what you know:

- He has claimed to use the moniker "Legoclones" for over a decade.
- There's a website, based on Star Wars lore, that he adopted and grew for about three years and now calls "his".

Find that website. The flag is its base URL.

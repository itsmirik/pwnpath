# Fruit Salad

Bobby really likes fruit. In fact, Bobby likes fruit so much that he made his
password out of **three fruit names in a row** - all lowercase, no spaces or
symbols.

Here is the SHA-1 hash of his password (`hash.txt`):

```
e23968e2f63c66616aa5f4fc1e9c45407717270a
```

Figure out his password.

Flag format: `ctf{password}`
